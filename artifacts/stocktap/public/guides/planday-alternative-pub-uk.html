Planday alternative for a UK pub: the real cost and what else fits

Shaun McManus Licensee, Washington, Tyne and Wear Prices checked 1 November 2026
Quick answer

Planday Starter is good value at £2.99 per user per month, but the Plus plan most pubs want has a £30 monthly fee on top and a 10-user minimum (checked 1 November 2026). For 20 staff that’s £59.80 on Starter or £109.80 on Plus.

Cheaper options: ShiftComplete (£29.80 for 20) or RotaCloud Standard (£40). For the cost of each day only, try the free pub rota maker.

I run a pub, and Planday is built with hospitality in mind, which shows. It’s a proper option. Landlords usually start looking elsewhere when they see the Plus plan’s monthly fee, or when they realise they’re billed in blocks of five. Here’s what Planday’s own pricing page said today.

A user is anyone with a login, billed in bundles of five (23 users is billed as 25). Monthly or quarterly billing. Paid onboarding available, and SMS messages are charged. 30-day free trial of Plus, no card. The page doesn’t say whether VAT is included.

What Planday does better than most

  • Built for shift-based businesses like pubs, with lots of UK hospitality customers.
  • Starter is one of the cheapest per-user prices from a big name.
  • Plus links your revenue so you can see wage cost against sales, and handles leave and absence properly.
  • Working time rule warnings even on Starter.

Why pubs look for an alternative

  • The £30 monthly fee on Plus. For a small team, that’s a big chunk of the bill.
  • Holiday management is on Plus, not Starter.
  • Five-user bundles. You pay for empty seats.
  • Extras. SMS charges and paid onboarding if you want help setting up.

List prices from each vendor’s own page on 1 November 2026. Deputy excludes taxes; the others didn’t say.

RotaCloud

Priced by headcount band, including you and your managers. 11 to 20 staff: Standard £40/month, Pro £60/month (pay yearly and it’s 10 months’ money). Clocking in is an add-on, £18/month at that size. 30-day trial, no payment details. Standard includes holidays and swaps, so it’s the closest match to Planday Plus for less, as long as you don’t need the revenue link (that’s RotaCloud Pro).

ShiftComplete

£1.49 per staff member per month, every feature, no per-site fee, no contract. Free for 60 days, no card. No payroll or payroll software link, by its own description, so hours go out as a spreadsheet.

Deputy

Per user per month, GBP excluding taxes. Lite £3.25 (annual) or £3.60 (monthly), Core £4.25 or £4.75, Pro £6.50 or £7.25. Minimum spend of £20 per invoice on monthly plans. Trial up to 31 days, no card.

Connecteam

Free Small Business plan for up to 10 users. Above that, the Operations hub (time clock and scheduling) starts at $29/month billed yearly or $35 monthly for the first 30 users. Shown in US dollars when I checked. 14-day trial, no card.

For multi-site groups, Rotaready and Bizimply are covered in the full comparison.

Common questions

How much does Planday cost for a pub?

On 1 November 2026 Planday’s page showed Starter at £2.99 per user per month (minimum 5 users) and Plus at £3.99 per user per month plus a £30 monthly subscription fee (minimum 10 users). For 20 staff that’s £59.80 or £109.80 a month.

Does Planday have a free trial?

Yes, 30 days of the Plus plan with no credit card details, per Planday’s pricing page.

Why does Planday bill in fives?

Planday says users are billed in bundles of five, so 23 users is billed as 25.

Is holiday management on Planday Starter?

Leave and absence management is listed on Plus, not Starter.

Can StockTap replace Planday?

Not if you rely on holiday booking, payroll reporting or shift swaps. StockTap is stocktaking first with a simple rota, clock in and out and a weekly wages as % of takings board.

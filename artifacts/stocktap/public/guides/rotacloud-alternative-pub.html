RotaCloud alternative for a pub: what it costs and what else fits

Shaun McManus Licensee, Washington, Tyne and Wear Prices checked 1 November 2026
Quick answer

RotaCloud is a good, fairly priced UK rota app, so make sure you actually need to leave. For 11 to 20 staff it’s £40/month on Standard, plus £18 if you want clocking in (checked 1 November 2026).

If price is the issue, ShiftComplete (£1.49 per staff member, clocking in included) and Planday Starter (£2.99 per user) came out cheaper for a 20-person pub. If you only want to see each day’s staff cost, the free pub rota maker does that for nothing.

I run a pub, and RotaCloud is one of the names that comes up every time landlords talk rotas in the Facebook groups. Usually the question is either “is it worth the money” or “what else is there”. So I checked RotaCloud’s own pricing page today and lined it up against the alternatives.

What RotaCloud charges (checked 1 November 2026)

RotaCloud prices by how many employees you have, and they ask you to count yourself and every admin and manager too. Pay yearly and you pay ten months’ worth.

30-day free trial, no payment info required. No set-up fees, per their page. The page doesn’t say whether VAT is included. SMS notifications are a further add-on.

What RotaCloud does better than most

  • Standard already includes holiday management, time off in lieu, sickness tracking, shift swapping, staff availability and daily, weekly and monthly labour costs.
  • Pro adds labour forecasting (staff costs as a percentage of sales) and budget caps while you schedule.
  • Unlimited sites on every plan, and free phone and live chat support from a UK team.
  • Published prices. You know what you’ll pay before you talk to anyone.

Why pubs look for an alternative

  • Clocking in costs extra. Timesheets and the payroll export sit in the Time & Attendance add-on.
  • Band jumps. Go from 20 to 21 people and Standard goes from £40 to £60 a month. Pubs carry a lot of casual weekend staff, so this bites.
  • Wages against takings is on Pro. If that’s the bit you care about, you’re on the dearer plan.
  • Some landlords only want the cost per day, not a full HR system.

List prices from each vendor’s own page on 1 November 2026. Deputy excludes taxes; the others didn’t say.

ShiftComplete

£1.49 per staff member per month, every feature, no per-site fee, no contract. Free for 60 days, no card. It does rotas from staff availability, shift swaps, holiday requests, clocking in and labour cost while you build the rota, plus temperature logs, allergens and daily checks. It doesn’t run payroll or link to EPOS or payroll software, by its own description.

Planday

Per user per month. Starter £2.99 (minimum 5 users). Plus £3.99 plus a £30 monthly subscription fee (minimum 10 users). Pro is custom. Billed in bundles of five users. 30-day trial of Plus, no card. Plus is the plan with leave management and revenue reporting, which is the closer match to RotaCloud Pro.

Deputy

Per user per month, GBP excluding taxes. Lite £3.25 (annual) or £3.60 (monthly), Core £4.25 or £4.75, Pro £6.50 or £7.25. Minimum spend of £20 per invoice on monthly plans. Trial up to 31 days, no card. Dearer than RotaCloud for most pubs, but Core gives you auto-scheduling and demand forecasting, which RotaCloud doesn’t list.

Connecteam

Free Small Business plan for up to 10 users. Above that, the Operations hub (time clock and scheduling) starts at $29/month billed yearly or $35 monthly for the first 30 users. Shown in US dollars when I checked. 14-day trial, no card.

Rotaready and Bizimply are also options if you run several sites. Both are demo-led; I’ve covered them in the full rota app comparison.

Common questions

How much is RotaCloud for a pub with 20 staff?

On 1 November 2026 RotaCloud’s page showed £40/month on Standard or £60/month on Pro for 11 to 20 employees, with Time & Attendance (clocking in) £18/month extra. Paying yearly costs ten months’ worth.

Does RotaCloud have a free plan?

No free plan was listed. There’s a 30-day free trial with no payment info required.

Is there a cheaper alternative to RotaCloud with clocking in included?

ShiftComplete lists £1.49 per staff member per month with clocking in included, which is £29.80 for 20 staff. Connecteam is free up to 10 users. Check what each one leaves out before you switch.

Does RotaCloud show wages as a percentage of sales?

Yes, on the Pro plan, which RotaCloud calls labour forecasting. Standard shows daily, weekly and monthly labour costs.

Can StockTap replace RotaCloud?

Only if all you use is a basic rota and a wage % figure. StockTap doesn’t do holiday booking, shift swaps, payroll or forecasting. If you rely on those, stay on RotaCloud or move to another full rota app.

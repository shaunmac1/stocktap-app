How to get your daily takings out of your till (Square, Zettle, SumUp, Epos Now and more)

Shaun McManus 30 September 2026

Every till can give you a spreadsheet of what you took each day. It's usually three or four clicks in the back office, on a laptop rather than the till itself. Paste it into StockTap's Daily Board, or pick the file, and your weeks, months and last-year comparisons fill straight in. Below is where the report lives on each of the tills pubs actually use, taken from each maker's own help pages, with the traps I'd want to know about first.

One thing to be straight about before the steps: StockTap brings in daily takings from a file today. It doesn't pull product-by-product sales from your till yet, so the till figure on a Spot Check still goes in by hand. When a till link is live, this page will say so.

What StockTap will read

  • One row per day. A date, then what you took. Extra columns are fine: if there's more than one money column (gross, net, VAT, discounts), StockTap lists them and you tap the one that's what you took.
  • Days across the top. Some tills put each day in its own column with rows like Gross sales, Tax and Total collected. StockTap reads that too, and you pick the row.
  • UK dates like 29/09/2026, 29-Sep-2026 or 2026-09-29, with or without a time on the end. Totals rows and headings are left out and it tells you what it left out.
  • It checks before it saves. You see how many days, the date range, the total and the biggest single day. If the biggest day looks daft, you've probably picked the wrong column.

Pick the figure including VAT, the same number you'd put on the Board at close. Where to find it: on the Daily Board, tap Bring in past takings, then paste or pick the CSV.

Square

Where: Square Dashboard on a computer: Reports > Sales > Sales summary. Set Summary to Daily, pick your dates, then the export button and Start export. You get a CSV.

Watch for: Square's daily export puts the days across the top, one per column. StockTap reads it that way round, so there's no need to flip it in Excel. Square's "Gross sales" is before VAT; the figure that includes VAT is Total collected, and that's the one StockTap picks first. Late-night trade counts towards whichever day your reporting hours start, set under Reports > Settings.

Zettle by PayPal

Where: my.zettle.com on a computer, Reports, pick the dates, then Export and Excel report. Zettle's help says the Go app can't export to Excel.

Watch for: Zettle shows sales by day on screen for ranges up to a month. I couldn't confirm from Zettle's own pages that the Excel file has one row per day, so export a month at a time and check the preview. "Total sales" includes VAT.

SumUp POS (the old Goodtill back office)

Where: Reports > Advanced Reports > Advanced Sales Report. Set the dates, set the report type to daily, Run Report, then CSV. If it won't run first time, Email Report sends it to you.

SumUp POS Pro

Where: back office, Reports tab, Accounting, Export, and choose Detailed CSV, which SumUp describes as split by calendar day.

Watch for: if your till closes after midnight, the detailed file puts each sale on its calendar day, midnight to midnight, which won't always match the night you think of it as.

SumUp card reader only (no POS)

The SumUp app and me.sumup.com show a daily revenue breakdown, but the download is a PDF. I couldn't find a CSV of daily takings for this setup, so here it's copy the figures across by hand, or type a month into a spreadsheet and paste that.

Epos Now

Where: Back Office on a computer, the grid icon at the top right, Reporting > Sales Report, choose Time Period, set your dates and the interval to daily, Apply, then CSV or Excel from the buttons at the bottom. Epos Now say exports only work from a laptop or computer.

Lightspeed Restaurant (K-Series)

Where: Back Office, Reports > Sales Summary, set the time interval to Daily, then Download as CSV or XLSX. Another route is Reports > All reports > Monthly Turnover, which splits a month by day with a gross or net switch.

Watch for: in the UK, Lightspeed's "net sales" has service charge taken off. Pick the gross figure for the Board.

Toast

Where: Toast Web, Reports > Sales > Sales summary, then the download icon for CSV or XLS.

Watch for: Toast counts a sale on the day the bill was opened, not paid, and only keeps 15 months online. I couldn't confirm the export has one row per day, so check the preview before you bring it in.

ICRTouch (TouchOffice Web)

TouchOffice Web exports reports as CSV, and product sales live under the PLU Sales reports. I couldn't find ICRTouch's own instructions for a one-row-per-day takings report. If your dealer set up a daily or Z report for you, export that as CSV and paste it in. TouchOffice Web and the till's Z read can differ if they cover different periods, so compare like with like.

Zonal (Aztec)

Aztec reports run in a Windows program on the office PC, not a website, and export as HTML, XLS or CSV from the toolbar. Zonal's recommended Statement of Trade is built to be viewed by the week, and I couldn't confirm a daily export from their guides. Zonal's CSV can include totals rows; StockTap leaves those out and tells you.

Tevalis

Tevalis reports live in Business Analytics, in the browser, with CSV and Excel export and reports that can be emailed to you on a schedule. Their release notes list sales reports broken down by day, but I couldn't find step-by-step instructions to link to, so ask Tevalis support which daily report to schedule.

Beanstore (Flooid)

Beanstore is a till system sold to big estates rather than single pubs, so if you're on it, the company that runs your estate chose it and controls the back office. I couldn't find anything public on what reports a tenant or partner can download. If you can see a daily sales report in whatever portal you're given, export it as CSV and paste it in. If you can't, ask whoever runs your estate, in writing, what you're entitled to see. If all you've got is the figure off the Z read each night, the Board takes that in two seconds.

If your till isn't here

Look for a sales, takings or trading report with a daily breakdown, export it as CSV or Excel, and paste it in. If the till only gives PDFs, copy the date and total columns into a spreadsheet first. Either way, StockTap shows you exactly what it's going to bring in before anything is saved.

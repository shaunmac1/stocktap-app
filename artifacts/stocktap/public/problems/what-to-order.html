How StockTap sorts it

Set a par level once

How much of each line you want on the shelf. After every count, StockTap lists what's below par, grouped by supplier, with the cost.

Send it in seconds

Copy the order into a text or email to your rep, or download it as a spreadsheet.

Use Venners? It goes further

Drop in your Venners audit and StockTap builds the order from what you actually sold, less what's left, plus the deliveries you've added since.

It orders in the way you buy

Kegs, casks, cases of 12, boxes of postmix, single bottles. Not millilitres you then have to convert.

You stay in charge

Tap + or − on any line before you send it. StockTap keeps showing what it suggested.

What you need

Your StockTap count and a par level for each line. If you use Venners, add their audit PDF for the fuller order list shown in the video.

The 1,000 Bottle Challenge

Landlords building the UK's biggest pub bottle-weight library, for landlords. Every bottle that goes in gets filled in for every pub that counts by weight, so nobody has to weigh it again. We're aiming for 1,000 bottles. Help us get there.

Have a look before you weigh anything. No point doing one we've already got.

    How it works

    1. Grab 10 full bottles off the shelf or out the cellar. They need to be sealed, never opened, cap still on.
    2. Pop each one on your kitchen scales and write down the weight in grams.
    3. Add them below with the size and what type of drink it is. Takes about 10 minutes all in.
    4. When a second pub's weight agrees with yours (within 5 grams) it goes straight into the library. If nobody else has that bottle yet, I'll check it myself.

    A thank you for your time

    Get 10 new bottles verified and you get a month of StockTap free. Get 20 verified and it's two months. That's the cap, two months.

    Verified means a second pub's weight agrees with yours, or I've checked it myself. Bottles we've already got don't count towards it, but thanks anyway.

What this does and doesn't count

It checks one thing: does a day bring in enough to cover the staff working it, after stock and VAT. It doesn't count rent, rates, energy or other bills, so a day can pass here and still not make you money overall. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere. Never cut below the cover you need to run the pub safely.

Staff cost includes holiday pay at 12.07% (the usual rate for irregular hours), employer National Insurance at 15% above £96.15 a week (none for under-21s), and 3% employer pension on earnings above £120 a week (auto-enrolment is for staff aged 22 and over earning over £10,000 a year). 2027 minimum wage rates from GOV.UK, 28 October 2026. NI and pension thresholds are the 2026/27 figures, which may change from April 2027.

How the cost is worked out

Each person's cost for the week is their pay plus holiday pay at 12.07% (the usual rate for irregular hours), employer National Insurance at 15% on weekly earnings above £96.15 (none for under-21s, or if you tick Employment Allowance), and 3% employer pension on earnings above £120 a week for staff 22 and over earning more than £10,000 a year (we use 21+ as a guide). That cost is then shared across the days they work. "Needs through the till" is the staff cost divided by what you keep of each £1 after VAT. It doesn't count rent, rates, energy or stock losses.

2027 minimum wage rates from GOV.UK, 28 October 2026. NI and pension thresholds are 2026/27 figures and may change from April 2027. A planning aid, not payroll or legal advice. Never go below the cover you need to run the pub safely.

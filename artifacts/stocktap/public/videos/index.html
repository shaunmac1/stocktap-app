How StockTap works

Sixteen short screen recordings from the real app, nothing staged. Start with the first two and you will have a stock value you can trust inside fifteen minutes.

Getting started

Set up in ten minutes

Pick your lines from the catalogue, no typing. Add the rest later.

Weigh your first bottle

Any kitchen scale that reads grams. Type the number, StockTap does the ml, tenths and pounds.

Your first stocktake

Count what you like, close it, get a value. Spirits by weight, bottles by number, kegs by pints.

Kegs and casks: set the size

Pin, firkin, kilderkin, barrel, 30L, 50L, 100L. Pick it once and pints turn into pounds.

Locations and reminders

Bar, cellar, beer garden. Count them separately and let the app remind you.

Every day

The daily spot check

Weigh a few busy lines, type what the till rang. The gap is your loss in measures and pounds.

The Daily Board

Type tonight’s take. Same day last week, last four, a year ago, all on one screen. Wages underneath.

Daily checks

Fridge temps, opening and closing checks, weekly jobs, refusals. The EHO diary on your phone.

Money

Scan a delivery note

One photo. Stock goes up, cost prices update, the bill lands in Finances as still payable.

Finances

Takings and bills in ten seconds, or import the sheet you already keep. Net, payable and VAT to reclaim.

Reports: where the money went

Valuation, GP by line, dead stock, order guide, and a baseline against your stocktaker.

Deals and offers

Running a double-up? Log it so a busy Friday is not read as a loss.

Team

Team and wages

Tap to clock in. Forgotten clock-outs flagged. Wage bill live against the take.

The rota

Bar and kitchen, day by day, with quick-fill shifts. Copy last week in one tap or photograph the paper one.

Staff access

Add a team member, give them a code. They never see the money.

On the staff phone

What your staff see: sign up, enter the code, clock in. Clock and Checks, nothing else.

Try it on your own bar

14 days of Pro free, no card. A kitchen scale and your phone are all you need.

Straight answers

Questions landlords ask

Does it work without special scales?

Yes. Any kitchen or bar scale works, and for most spirits your phone does the maths from a single weight. There's no proprietary hardware to buy or rent — that's the whole point.

How is weighing more accurate than tapping or eyeballing?

Tap-to-guess estimates how full a bottle looks. A weight measures what's actually there, to the gram, and StockTap converts it to millilitres and money. You can start on the quick method you know and switch to weighing when you want the accuracy.

Will my staff be able to see the takings or wages?

No. Staff clock in and do their checks on their own phones, but takings, wages and all financials are locked to admins — enforced in the database, not just hidden on screen.

Can I bring my existing spreadsheets in?

Yes. The Finances module imports the takings, spending and supplier sheets you already keep — upload a CSV or paste straight from Excel or Google Sheets, and StockTap maps the columns for you.

Is it really just for pubs?

No — it's built pub-first, but bars, restaurants, cafés and hotels run the same nightly cash-up, compliance and stock jobs. Same engine, same daily habit.

What does a stocktaker cost compared to this?

A single professional stocktake visit typically costs more than months of StockTap — and only tells you what you've already lost. StockTap is a daily habit that helps you stop losing it.
